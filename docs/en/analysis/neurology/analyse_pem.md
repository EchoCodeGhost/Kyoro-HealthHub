# PEM Evidence Score — Analyse und Visualisierung

> GENERATED FROM DOCSTRING — DO NOT EDIT BY HAND.  
> Source: `scripts/analysis/neurology/analyse_pem.py`

**Evidence tier:** calibrated (literature-based + parameters tuned to personal baselines, no external validation)

## Purpose

Reads from pem_evidence_scores (generated by compute_pem.py) and visualises score time series, recovery pattern distribution, monthly PEM burden and sport-adjusted PEM rate before/after the configured event date.

## Relevance

Enables neurological analysis, essential for nervous system diagnostics

## Method

Aggregation and visualisation of the pre-computed pem_evidence_scores table; breakdown by confidence level (confirmed = reaction was measured, regardless of threshold, vs. no_reaction_data = no measurement available); period comparison relative to infection_date.

## Data flow

- **Reads:** `pem_evidence_scores`
- **Writes:** `analyses/postinfectious/pem_*.{md,png}`

## Limitations

Quality depends on upstream compute_pem.py output; PEM score is heuristic, not clinically validated; sport-adjusted PEM rate requires sufficient training log data.
